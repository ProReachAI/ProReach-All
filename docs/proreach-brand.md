# ProReach brand source of truth

## Canonical identity

- **Brand and product name:** ProReach
- **Website:** https://www.proreach.in
- **Category:** Approval-first AI marketing workspace
- **Primary audience:** Founders, indie builders, lean marketing teams, and product marketers
- **Core promise:** Turn product truth into coordinated, channel-native social campaigns while keeping people in control of approval and publishing.

## What ProReach does

ProReach stores reusable context for each customer project: audience, positioning, proof, voice, goals, and claim guardrails. It uses that source of truth to create coordinated campaigns, adapt drafts for each channel, generate branded visuals, support review and editing, and publish immediately or on a schedule after human approval.

Current product channels are Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Threads, X, and LinkedIn, subject to each provider's account, API, permission, review, and pricing requirements.

## Brand relationship model

```text
ProReach — platform and central public brand
└── Customer workspace
    ├── Customer project: Prophrase
    │   ├── Prophrase product context and campaigns
    │   └── Prophrase-owned social accounts
    └── Customer project: Another product
        ├── That product's context and campaigns
        └── That product's social accounts
```

Prophrase is a customer/project, not another name for ProReach. Its fixtures may appear in tests and examples because ProReach must support customer brands, but its identity must never be used for ProReach's central website, developer applications, company pages, or social profiles.

## Central account rules

- Central profile display name: **ProReach**
- Preferred social handle to attempt: **@ProReachHQ**
- Fallback handles: **@GetProReach**, then **@UseProReach**
- `@ProReach` is already owned by an unrelated X user.
- Central website link: `https://www.proreach.in`
- Central accounts publish ProReach product education, founder insights, campaign teardowns, release notes, approved proof, and company updates.
- Customer content appears centrally only with permission as a case study, testimonial, or clearly labeled example.
- Every customer connects its own provider account to its own ProReach project. Do not reuse central ProReach credentials for a customer project.

## Product proof that can be stated

- Reusable project context for audience, positioning, proof, voice, goals, and constraints
- Coordinated campaign generation instead of disconnected one-off posts
- Channel-specific drafts for supported networks
- Editable copy, CTAs, hashtags, and visual direction
- Human review and approval before publishing
- Immediate and scheduled publishing workflows
- Project-scoped provider grants and publishing destinations
- Encrypted OAuth tokens and replay-resistant authorization sessions

## Claims that must not be made without evidence

- Guaranteed reach, followers, engagement, leads, conversions, revenue, or search rankings
- Fully autonomous publishing without user approval
- Support for a social channel or media format not implemented in the product
- Customer performance results without permission and verifiable data
- Platform certification, partnership, or endorsement that has not been granted

## Naming check before publishing or setup

Before creating an account, developer app, landing page, campaign, or public document, confirm:

1. Is this for the ProReach company/platform or for a customer project?
2. If central, every public identity field says ProReach and links to `proreach.in`.
3. If customer-specific, the customer's name, assets, accounts, permissions, and project scope remain separate.
4. The copy does not imply that a customer is the platform owner or that ProReach owns a customer's accounts.
