# ProReach creative system

ProReach treats social media as product advertising, not generic image generation. Every visual must pass a three-second test: a new viewer should understand both the customer's tension and what the product changes before reading the caption.

## Creative archetypes

The creative director rotates among these concepts and avoids recently used styles:

- **Product UI stage:** a recognizable product workflow or truthful blank UI structure is the hero.
- **Product outcome hero:** one visible outcome dominates the frame.
- **Before/after contrast:** the customer's specific friction changes into the product-enabled result.
- **Three-beat workflow:** input, mechanism, and outcome appear in one readable flow.
- **Feature orbit system:** a central product engine connects to a few real capabilities.
- **Toolkit grid:** real product capabilities form a bold modular system.
- **Cinematic 3D symbol:** one direct product-mechanism symbol is staged with premium depth.
- **Signal versus noise:** confusing inputs collapse into one clear actionable output.
- **Faceless character story:** a clearly animated, toy-like character demonstrates the situation without a real face.
- **Kinetic type world:** exact application-rendered typography and simple product shapes create the scroll stop.

## Hard constraints

- No real people, realistic faces, skin, hands, portraits, stock photography, celebrity likenesses, or uncanny anatomy.
- No generic robots, brains, glowing `AI` letters, circuit-board wallpaper, random thread/rope, floating cubes, or unexplained futuristic rooms.
- No generated product name, logo, readable UI copy, metrics, testimonials, integrations, or unsupported capabilities.
- Do not literalize a word from the hook when the object does not explain the product.
- One dominant idea per creative; product relevance outranks decoration.

## Rendering pipeline

1. Campaign generation creates a product-grounded hook, post, CTA, media format, and frame script.
2. The creative director selects a non-recent archetype and states the visible product mechanism.
3. FLUX creates only the 4:5 background scene with a protected typography zone.
4. ProReach adds exact English copy, the uploaded logo, CTA, and deterministic product graphics.
5. Carousel and motion renderers reuse the same creative archetype, so the campaign varies without losing brand consistency.

Static posts and carousels use a 4:5 feed composition. Motion uses a 4:5 three-beat loop: tension, product mechanism, outcome/CTA. Native Instagram Reel publishing still requires an MP4 encoder; the current motion renderer produces an animated GIF for supported destinations and previewing.
